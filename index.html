What this site can see

  • With your permission, it reads your To Do lists and your name. Microsoft doesn't let it see anything else.
  • Everything happens in this browser tab. There's no server and no database, so your tasks never reach the people who made this.
  • If you choose Google Tasks, it can add lists and tasks to your Google account. It never deletes anything.
  • No ads, no tracking, no cookies. Read the privacy page, or read the code.

Questions

Will this change anything in Microsoft To Do?

No. It only asks Microsoft for permission to read. If you choose to include attached files, Microsoft makes it ask for wider permission, because that's the only way Microsoft shares them, but the page still only reads. Your To Do lists stay exactly as they are until Microsoft closes the app.

Why does Microsoft call this app "unverified"?

Microsoft only gives its "verified" badge to registered companies. This is a free project made by an individual, so it shows as unverified. The code is public, so anyone can check what it does.

What doesn't move to Google Tasks?

Google Tasks has no repeats, reminders, importance or attachments. Those details are written into each task's notes, and kept in full in the backup. Microsoft doesn't share My Day or list groups with other apps, so nothing can copy those.

Can I use a work or school account?

Usually. Some organisations block apps they haven't approved. If yours does, Microsoft will say an administrator needs to approve it. Microsoft is moving work and school accounts to Planner.

What about lists that other people share with me?

They're included. The people on a shared list don't come along, so share the new list with them again in your new app.

Is it really free?

Yes, with no catch. If it helps you, a star on GitHub helps other people find it, and a tip keeps it going.
